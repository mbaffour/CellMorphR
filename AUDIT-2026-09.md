# Audit 2026-09 — disposition

The September 2026 statistical and software audit of CellMorphR v3.1.2 raised 22
findings (CM-1 … CM-22). This file records what was done about each one. The
fixes are released as **3.2.0**; see [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md) for the
user-facing description and the commit history of branch `claude/audit-2026-09`
for the code.

**Headline:** the two global models leaked and are corrected. The pairwise path —
the one the tool is named for and the one most users run — was measured correct
throughout and is unchanged in substance.

## Measured type-I error, before and after

Null data: 3 dishes per condition × 2 conditions × 5 timepoints × 100 cells,
true condition effect exactly zero, nominal α = 0.05. "Before" figures are the
auditor's; "after" figures are from `tests/testthat/test-global-models.R` and
`test-replication-unit.R` on this branch.

| Test | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Naive cell-level Welch t-test (pseudoreplicated reference) | 90.8% | 90.8% (unchanged — kept as the control) |
| Pairwise, Welch on replicate medians | 2.8% | 2.8% |
| Global ANOVA — Condition | **41.0%** | 6.2% (6.3% with dish drift) |
| Global ANOVA — Time | 0.0% | 2.5% (6.0% with dish drift) |
| Global ANOVA — Condition × Time | 0.0% | 6.2% (5.0% with dish drift) |
| LMM — Condition | 5.0% | 3.3% (5.3% with dish drift) |
| LMM — Time (with dish drift) | **99.2%** | 4.0% |
| LMM — Condition × Time (with dish drift) | **100.0%** | 4.7% |

The "after" figures sit within binomial tolerance of 5% at the simulation counts
used; the suite asserts that rather than the point estimates.

## What a reviewer should still check

- **Nothing was verified through the running Shiny app.** The audit drove the
  statistical core directly, and so does the test suite: everything above the
  `END OF STATISTICAL CORE` marker in `CellMorphR_v3.R` is plain R with no
  reactivity. The reactive wiring around the corrected fits — which tab reads
  which reactive, invalidation order, the 13 `downloadHandler`s — is reviewed by
  reading only. Launch the app and click through the Statistics, Effect Sizes and
  Multi-Feature tabs before release.
- **The presentation findings are untouched.** Conditions are still coloured by
  `scales::hue_pal()` and a fixed blue/red pair at n = 2, neither of which is
  colourblind-safe (CM-11).
- **Power, not just type-I.** The corrected models test Condition on 4
  denominator degrees of freedom where the old one used 16. That is the honest
  number, and it is a large loss of nominal power; users accustomed to the old
  output will see previously "significant" results disappear. This is the
  intended consequence of the fix, not a regression, but it deserves a line in
  the release announcement.
- **Sphericity** is assumed and not corrected for in the repeated-measures ANOVA
  (no Greenhouse–Geisser / Huynh–Feldt adjustment). Noted as a limitation in the
  README; worth revisiting if long time courses become common.
- **Simulation assumptions.** The type-I figures above come from Gaussian cell
  values in a balanced design with additive dish offsets. Real morphometry is
  right-skewed and often unbalanced. The direction of the original findings was
  structural and would not reverse, but the exact percentages would shift.
